A gentle Knight was pricking on the plaine, ycladd in mightie armes and silver shielde, wherein old dints of deepe wounds did remaine, the cruell markes of many a bloudy fielde; yet armes till that time did he never wield: his angry steede did chide his foming bitt, as much disdayning to the curbe to yield: full jolly knight he seemd, and faire did sitt, as one for knightly giusts and fierce encounters fitt.

Upon his brest a bloudie Crosse he bore, the deare remembrance of his dying Lord, for whose sweete sake that glorious badge he wore, and dead as living ever him ador'd: upon his shielde the like was also scor'd, for soveraine hope, which in his helpe he had: right faithfull true he was in deede and word, but of his cheere did seeme too solemne sad.
