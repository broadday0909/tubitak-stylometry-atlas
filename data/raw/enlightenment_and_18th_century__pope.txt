Know then thyself, presume not God to scan; the proper study of mankind is man. Placed on this isthmus of a middle state, a being darkly wise, and rudely great: with too much knowledge for the sceptic side, with too much weakness for the Stoic's pride, he hangs between; in doubt to act, or rest; in doubt to deem himself a God, or beast.

What dire offence from am'rous causes springs, what mighty contests rise from trivial things, I sing—This verse to Caryl, Muse! is due: this, ev'n Belinda may vouchsafe to view: slight is the subject, but not so the praise, if she inspire, and he approve the lays. Say what strange motive, Goddess! could compel a well-bred Lord to assault a gentle Belle?
