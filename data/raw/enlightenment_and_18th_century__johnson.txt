Ye who listen with credulity to the whispers of fancy, and pursue with eagerness the phantoms of hope; who expect that age will perform the promises of youth, and that the deficiencies of the present day will be supplied by the morrow; attend to the history of Rasselas, prince of Abyssinia.

Human life is everywhere a state in which much is to be endured, and little to be enjoyed. Such is the state of man, that none can be said to be happy; for happiness requires freedom from pain, and an uninterrupted flow of pleasures; but nature has subjected us to pain, and wisdom forbids us to expect pleasure without alloy.
